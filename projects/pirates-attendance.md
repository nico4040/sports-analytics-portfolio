# Skenes, Bobbleheads, and Pride Nights
### Determinants of Pittsburgh Pirates' Attendance

[Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [Read the published article](https://doi.org/10.1177/15586235261499115)

**Authors:** Nicholas C. Koch and E. Frank Stephenson  
**Journal:** International Journal of Sport Finance, published online in 2026

## Research question

How are Paul Skenes's starts and different promotions associated with Pittsburgh Pirates home attendance?

## Approach

The paper studies game-level attendance from the 2022–2025 seasons. Regression specifications use attendance in levels and logs, accounting for promotions, opponents, timing, team performance, precipitation, and local economic conditions.

## Findings and relevance

Skenes starts were associated with approximately 3,300 additional fans per game. Traditional promotions, including bobbleheads and fireworks, also had positive estimated attendance relationships. Pride Nights had a negative estimated relationship in this sample.

The study connects player visibility and promotional scheduling to fan demand. Its findings are specific to the Pirates and the studied years; they should not be treated as universal effects or as evidence of individual fans' motivations. Observational estimates also depend on the model and available controls.
